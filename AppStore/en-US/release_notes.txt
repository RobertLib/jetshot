Steadier iCloud sync, a Slow Motion that slows the whole fight, hitboxes that match what you see, and a long list of fixes.

• iCloud progress you can trust: a new device played offline no longer loses its progress when its first sync brings in an old reset, a device that missed a reset can no longer bring erased progress back, and switching Apple IDs no longer overwrites progress on your other devices. The menu and the level select now catch up the moment a sync lands — no padlocks on levels cleared on another device, and ENDLESS appears without leaving the menu.
• Slow Motion now slows the whole hostile side. New waves, walls and asteroids used to keep arriving at full rate, so the screen filled twice as fast while it ran.
• Hitboxes match what is drawn: asteroids, spinners and wave walls are solid exactly where you see them, and a boss's wall of fire can only be passed through its gap — no more safe lane at the edge.
• Every boss now opens with its signature attack, so the first move tells you which machine you are facing.
• Freeze Bomb: frozen enemies stay frozen after a phone call or Control Center, no longer get stuck in a white flash, and a teleporter caught mid-blink is fully visible.
• Lightning strikes only enemies on screen, Homing Missiles no longer hover inside a phased ghost, a sniper's lock-on stays in step with its shot under Slow Motion, and formations no longer fire from above the top of the screen.
• Fairer endings: rounds still in flight when your ship goes down no longer score or finish off a boss, and a ship left inside a wall when its shield runs out now takes the hit.
• An enemy left with no path to fly no longer stays on screen and holds up the end of the level.
• Scores are grouped by thousands everywhere, the HUD and the BEST line included, and a long score shrinks to fit. An endless run now ends in RUN OVER instead of LEVEL FAILED.
• Controls: a drag that strays onto the bottom edge no longer sends you to the home screen mid-run, a finger moved under the pause menu no longer jerks the ship on RESUME, and the menu ignores taps until its buttons have appeared.
• Smoother and lighter: fewer hitches mid-wave, steadier spinning wireframes, less memory, music that recovers if the system's audio restarts, and no white flash at launch. On the home screen the app is now just "Jetshot".
• Coin formations keep their shape as they fall, the magnet leaves coins you have already collected alone, split asteroids fly apart both ways, and swaying walls stay on screen.

No account or sign-in is required. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no
third-party SDKs of any kind — the app links only Apple frameworks (UIKit,
SpriteKit, AVFoundation, CoreImage, Foundation). There is no networking code, no
StoreKit and no advertising identifier.

Progression: only level 1 is unlocked at the start, and each level unlocks the
next. ENDLESS appears on the main menu once level 1 has been cleared — it is a
run with no completion condition and needs no unlocking beyond that. To reach the
later levels and their bosses without playing through fifty of them, use RESET on
the level-select screen and note that any level already completed stays
selectable from the grid.

Controls: drag a finger anywhere on the screen to fly the ship. The guns fire by
themselves; there is no fire button. Tap the pause button at the top for the
pause menu and settings (music, sound and haptics, each switchable on its own).

Data: the game stores progress (completed levels, scores, star ratings, weapon
loadouts and the endless records) in UserDefaults and mirrors it to
NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore so that it follows the player's own iCloud account
across their devices. That is the only thing that ever leaves the device, it goes
to the user's own iCloud and nowhere else, and the app collects nothing. The
iCloud key-value entitlement in jetshot.entitlements is there for exactly this.
With no iCloud account signed in the game works normally against local storage.

Age rating: 4+. The game is an abstract arcade shooter — the ships, enemies and
explosions are drawn as geometric vector shapes, there are no characters, no
blood, no realistic violence, no chat, no user-generated content and no links out
of the app.

Localisation: English and Czech, switched by the device language.

The app supports iPhone and iPad, portrait only.
